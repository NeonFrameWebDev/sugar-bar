Pat's slice of heaven on Main Street

Pat opened Sugar Bar to do one thing well: pour drinks that taste like the kind of fun you forgot you were allowed to have. No mixology lectures. No tiny garnishes. Just sugary, creative cocktails in a room that feels like Stevens Point pulled up a stool next to you.

Tucked into a brick storefront downtown, Sugar Bar is small on purpose. The Packers are on, the music's good, and the gummy bear martini is what people drive up from out of town for. As one regular put it:

"I have made trips to Stevens Point for this reason alone."

Cheers.

Inside the bar

Three stories of historic Stevens Point brick on the outside, warm low light and a Packers helmet inside. It's a small room. That's the point. Sit at the bar, watch Pat work, hear the playlist do its thing. Friday and Saturday it gets loud in the best way; Tuesday through Thursday it's the kind of place you can actually have a conversation in.
